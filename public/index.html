About

About Load Smart

Load Smart is a freight-matching portal that connects Shippers (who need goods moved), Carriers (who own trucks and move the goods) and Brokers (who arrange transport on a shipper's behalf) in one place, with every account KYC-reviewed before it goes live.

How the portal works

  • Registration & review — Shipper, Broker, and Carrier accounts each register with their business/KYC details. An admin reviews every submission and marks it Accepted, Pending, or Rejected before shipper-side tools unlock.
  • Shipper workflow — once accepted, a shipper can get an instant freight-rate estimate, book a shipment directly, or send a manual rate request for a carrier/broker to quote against.
  • Carrier & Broker workflow — registered carriers and brokers appear as selectable providers on a shipper's rate request, and can be assigned to a shipment by the admin team.
  • Request / rate workflow — a shipper's rate request goes to Admin, who can accept it as-is or send back a counter rate; the shipper then accepts or rejects that counter, and the final agreed rate is recorded either way.
  • Tracking workflow — every booking or accepted rate request gets a unique Token/Order number. Admin updates its live location, status, and progress, and the shipper watches it update in real time on their Live Tracking dashboard.

Aadhaar numbers submitted at registration are checked for a valid checksum format only — this is not a verification against UIDAI's official database, and Load Smart does not claim any government affiliation, approval, or certification.
